Our featured main event — keynotes, showcases, and networking for students, faculty, and industry guests.

Event Overview

Main Event - Tech

Annual Tech Symposium 2026

Date: November 15, 2026

Time: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Venue: Main Auditorium Hall A

Join industry leaders, student innovators, and faculty for a full day of keynote sessions, live project showcases, panel discussions, and hands-on demo zones. The Annual Tech Symposium brings together all departments to present research, startups, and final-year projects, plus career networking with tech partners hiring for 2027 internships.

Register Now

Agenda

  1. 09:00 AM: Registration & Welcome Coffee
  2. 10:00 AM: Opening Keynote - Future of AI on Campus
  3. 11:30 AM: Student Project Showcase Part 1
  4. 01:00 PM: Lunch Break & Networking
  5. 02:00 PM: Panel: Careers in Tech & Engineering
  6. 03:30 PM: Live Demos & Innovation Lab Tour
  7. 04:30 PM: Awards & Closing Remarks

Tickets & Entry

  • Students: Free entry with valid student ID
  • Faculty & Staff: Free entry, registration required
  • External Guests: $10 at the door

Seats are limited to 500. Doors open at 8:30 AM. Please bring your confirmation email and student ID.
